# Distinct card parameters, tribute summons and normal spells

Ticket [42] (#99) of the Phase G roadmap #94 (checkpoint #88 decisions) adds one engine rule, distinct card parameters, and finishes the Duel Ruleset's reduced rules: tribute summons, normal spells and the first real catalog.

**Distinct card parameters.** The card params of one Declared Action are distinct: binding one Card Instance to two of them is the typed Rejection `CardBoundTwice { param, card, first }`, where `param` is the later param and `first` the earlier one that already holds the card. The input GameState is untouched and there are no Events, as for any Rejection. The check is in the binding of a Declared Action's arguments (`arguments::bind`), per param in order, right after `UnknownCard` and before the owner, zone, card type and `where` checks of that param: the order is the argument count, then for each param the unknown card, `CardBoundTwice`, the owner, the zone, the card type, the `where`. It applies to every ruleset, with no opt-out, and to Declared Actions only (a phase's or a window's): a Card Choice binds one param at a time (ADR-0034) and an Activate binds only `self` (ADR-0038), so neither is affected. The listing is unchanged: it still builds every way to fill the params and keeps what `transition` accepts (ADR-0018, ADR-0021), so a candidate that repeats a card is in `listing_with_rejections` with `CardBoundTwice` and is not a Legal Action; no pruning of `fillings`. The Small, Effects and Response Rulesets change no Legal Action: the only Action of theirs with two card params is `attack`, whose `attacker` is the acting Player's and whose `target` is an opponent's card, so no card can fill both legally (declaring `(A, A)` is refused with `CardBoundTwice` where it was refused with `NotOpponentCard`; nothing in a Golden Game, snapshot or pin does that). The Duel's `attack` is the same case: its test now expects `CardBoundTwice` for `(attacker, attacker)` and checks `NotOpponentCard` with another own card.

**Tribute summons** (Duel Ruleset v3, `meta.version: 4`, the catalog's `ruleset_version` too). A monster of level 5 or 6 needs 1 tribute, one of level 7 or more needs 2; a tribute is a monster of the acting Player's Monster Zone, sent to the Graveyard as the cost. They are separate Actions whose `where` reads the level: `tribute_summon_one` (level 5 to 6; params `card`, `tribute`), `tribute_summon_two` (level 7 and up; `card`, `tribute_1`, `tribute_2`), and `tribute_set_one` and `tribute_set_two` (the same, in defence position). The `card` is a monster of the hand whose level is in the band, with `player.normal_summoned` false (the once-per-Turn flag covers every summon and set) and room: `lte: [{ sub: [{ count: { zone: monster_zone, player: active } }, N] }, 2]` with `N` the number of tributes. The capacity is arithmetic because a param `where` is evaluated before the costs (ADR-0016): "checked after the tributes leave" is `count - N <= 2`, so a summon with 2 tributes from a full Monster Zone is legal. The `cost` moves each tribute to `graveyard` in param order, and the effects are those of `normal_summon` and `set_monster` (move to the Monster Zone, set the flag, the position, `position_fixed`). The two orders of two tributes, `(card, A, B)` and `(card, B, A)`, are both listed: they differ in the Graveyard order, since the moves follow param order. No deduplication; it would need the listing to know which params are interchangeable.

**The Spell/Trap Zone and normal spells.** A zone `spell_trap_zone` (public, unordered; capacity 3 by `count`) and a card type `spell` (`name`, `effects: true`, no instance state). `play_spell` (Main only) takes a spell of the hand while the zone holds at most 2, its `cost` moves the card to the Spell/Trap Zone and its `effects` are empty. The spell's own `enter` trigger, `on: { enter: spell_trap_zone }`, runs when the cost's `CardMoved` is scanned: every `do` starts with `move: { card: self, to: graveyard }` and then does the effect, so the spell leaves the zone first. That order matters: `breaking_bolt` then asks its controller (`choose_card`, `owner: opponent`, the Monster Zone) for a monster and moves it to the Graveyard, and with no Candidate the rest of the `do` is skipped (ADR-0034), which would leave the spell in the zone forever had the move come after. `lucky_find` draws 2 cards, `mending_light` adds 1000 to its controller's `life_points`. The ruleset declares `effects: { active_zone: spell_trap_zone }`, required for an `enter` trigger to be eligible (ADR-0023, ADR-0024); monsters have `effects: false`, so declaring it changes nothing but spell triggers. Phase H, with face-down cards and traps, may revisit this choice. The main deck input admits `[monster, spell]`.

**The catalog and decks.** The sixteen monsters of level 1 to 4 stay; four of level 5 to 6 (`ashen_minotaur`, `tempest_roc`, `obsidian_golem`, `dune_wyrm`, ATK 2000 to 2400), three of level 7 to 8 (`abyss_leviathan`, `sun_colossus`, `storm_titan`, ATK 2600 to 3000) and the three spells are added. Each sample deck is 40 cards, at most 3 copies: 26 monsters of level 1 to 4 (all sixteen, deck A mostly the lower half twice, deck B the higher half), 6 of level 5 to 6, 2 of level 7 to 8 and 6 spells (2 of each). Over Seeds 0..100 the Duel self-play, every Invariant holding, ends 87 Games on `life_out` and 13 on `deck_out`, no drawn Game, the longest 218 Actions; every Game holds a tribute summon or set and 97 hold a spell. The Duel pin, coverage counts and Golden Game stay ticket [43]'s.

**Conflicts, amended in place.** ADR-0015 (the order of the binding Rejections gains `CardBoundTwice`), ADR-0016 (the binding checks per param), ADR-0018 (the listing never offers a repeated card), and ADR-0041, ADR-0042 and ADR-0043, wherever they say the Duel has a deck of monsters only, has no Active Zone, or has [42] still to come, each carrying an `Amended by ADR-0044` marker where it stands. ADR-0038 (the Rejection order of an Activate), ADR-0022 and ADR-0023 need no change: an Activate binds only `self`, a spell is an ordinary triggered Card Effect on an `effects: true` card type, whose `do` the engine already runs (ADR-0034's Choice included).

**Status**: accepted
