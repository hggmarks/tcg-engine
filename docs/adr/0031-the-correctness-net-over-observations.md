# The correctness net over Observations

Amended by ADR-0037: the Relabelling maps the cards of Stack Items and of the three item Events, and the Recomputed GameState difference has a Stack branch.

The harness of ADR-0020 and ADR-0026 now also checks what each Viewer observes (ticket [26], #66, of the Observations roadmap #61): three Invariants for every Viewer, observed Golden Games, and the measured cost of observing. The Uniform Random Policy, the Step Cap, the use of the public API only and `FailureKind` (unchanged) are ADR-0020's. `Observations`, the Observer and the Events are ADR-0028 to ADR-0030's, untouched; no file under `rulesets/` and nothing in `src/` changes.

**Three Invariants, after the nine.** `Invariant` gains `ObservationsStayConsistent`, `ObservationsIgnoreHiddenCards` and `ObservedActionsRoundTrip`, appended after `RejectionLeavesStateIntact` in this order. Each is checked for every Viewer (`Viewer::Player`, seats in order) at the Opening Position and after every transition, in `self_play` and in `verify_record`.

- *Observations stay consistent*, `check_observed_consistency(viewer, previous, events, next)`: `previous.apply_events(events)` must equal `next`. The detail names the Observed Event that does not apply, or the first part that differs (Turn Number, Active Player, phase, outcome *(Amended by ADR-0033: the Pending Decision, after the outcome)*, a Player's zone and its count or cards, a Visible Card and its owner, definition or state).
- *Observations ignore hidden cards*, `check_hidden_cards_ignored(observed, relabelled)`: the real Observed Step and the Observed Step of the Relabelled Game must be equal. The detail names the first part that differs, in this order: the Observed Event at index i, the Taken Action, a part of the Observed GameState, a Derived Read (handle and field).
- *Observed Actions round-trip*, `check_observed_actions(ruleset, observer, state, legal)`: `observed_legal_actions` translated back with `translate_action` must equal `legal` filtered to the Viewer's Player, in the same order and of the same length; the detail names the first index that differs. An `ObservationError` from either call is the violation. `legal` is the harness's own listing of the GameState (the one the policy draws from next; for an ended GameState, `legal_actions`, which is empty), listed once and reused.

**The Viewer is reported.** `InvariantViolation` gains `viewer: Option<Viewer>`, `None` for the nine engine Invariants. A step reports at most one violation per (Invariant, Viewer), ordered by Invariant and then by seat. This changes ADR-0020's "at most one violation per Invariant" (marked there). An `ObservationError` while a Viewer follows the Game is an `ObservationsStayConsistent` violation (as ADR-0026 made a failed fold a `DerivedReadsDoNotLeak` violation) and the harness stops observing that Viewer for the rest of the Game. A failure carries the Seed, the Action index, the Viewer and a reproducing record as before; an observation violation after Action index k comes with the record cut after k, and verifying it fails the same way. The round-trip check of a GameState runs right after the rejection checks of its listing in `play`, and right after the step checks in `verify_record`, which now lists each GameState for it.

**`Checks::EngineOnly`.** `Checks` gains `EngineOnly`: the nine engine Invariants without the observation checks. `On` is now all twelve. `EngineOnly` lists with rejections, as `On` does (ADR-0026, which is marked there). It exists only so that the example can measure the cost of observing. The same Seed gives the same Game under `On`, `EngineOnly` and `Off`, and a test asserts it on both rulesets.

**Observed Step.** What one Viewer observes at one step: `viewer`, the Observed Events (of the creation trace, or of the transition), the Taken Action (`None` at the Opening Position), the Observed GameState and the Derived Reads, one for every int field of every Visible Card, in handle order and catalog order, through `derived_read`. The harness keeps, per Viewer, an Observer and its Observed GameState: at the Opening Position `observer(viewer, empty, [])`, `follow_events` over the creation trace, `observe`; then `follow_transition` per Action. The Observed Step of the leak check is that incremental one, never a second fold.

**Hidden Cards.** The cards of a Viewer that were never in a zone it sees after any Event of the Game so far, the step's own trace included: a card is seen when a `CardCreated` puts it in a seen zone, or a `CardMoved` has a seen `from` or `to`. The harness computes them from the Events and `Zone::visibility()` with ADR-0028's meaning (`public` everyone, `owner` that zone's Player, `hidden` nobody), not from the Observer, so the check does not trust what it tests. A card seen in the middle of a trace is not hidden, which matters because `CardMovedIntoSight` carries the definition.

**The Relabelling.** Per step and per Viewer, *(Amended by ADR-0037: it also renames the bound cards of the three item Events (`ItemPushed`, `ItemResolved`, `ItemFizzled`), and a Pass names no card)* built fresh, over the creation trace, every trace so far and the step's Action, with the explicit set of cards to relabel (the Hidden Cards in the harness; a test passes seen cards to see what a leak looks like): 1. *definitions* are shuffled among those cards (instance states stay with their instance); 2. *ids* are shuffled within the classes of those cards created in the creation trace with the same owner and the same creation zone, which keeps ids dense in creation order and a moved card in its owner's zone; each `CardCreated` keeps its position and id and takes the definition and state of the card renamed to that id, every other Event and the Action's arguments map ids through the permutation *(Amended by ADR-0033: the Paused Resolution of a `DecisionPending` names cards too, its trigger, its queue and the Events it carries, and its ids go through the same permutation; an Answer names no card *(Amended by ADR-0034: an option Answer names none; the card of a Card Answer goes through the permutation in the Action and in its `DecisionAnswered`, and so do the cards of the `bound` names of a Paused Resolution)*)*; 3. in each `ZoneShuffled` of a zone the Viewer cannot see, the positions that hold those cards are shuffled among themselves. The randomness is one `RandomState::from_seed(Seed(seed.0 ^ RELABEL_STREAM))` per Game (`RELABEL_STREAM`, a documented `pub const`, like `POLICY_STREAM`), drawn in a fixed order (per step, the Viewers in seat order, then 1, 2 with the classes by smallest id, 3 with the Events in order) with Fisher-Yates. It never touches the GameState's or the policy's generator, so `verify_record` reproduces the same Relabelling, which a test pins call by call.

**Known limit: the Random State.** `ZoneShuffled.random_state` is not relabelled, so the leak check does not cover a leak of the Random State. ADR-0010 forbids that leak, and the Observed types hold no Random State by construction (ADR-0028, ADR-0029).

**The Relabelled Game.** *(Amended by ADR-0037: an Observed Stack Item is part of the Observed Step, so a leak through a bound card would show)* The relabelled Events are folded from `GameCreation::empty` (`apply_trace`), a fresh Observer from the Empty GameState follows all the relabelled Events before the step, and `follow_transition` follows the relabelled Action and step; that yields the relabelled Observed Step, which must equal the real one. A relabelled fold that does not apply, or an `ObservationError` on the relabelled side, is an `ObservationsIgnoreHiddenCards` violation naming the error. The cost is O(steps squared) per Game. It was measured, not optimised (below).

**Non-public Active Zone.** A Derived Read adds the Field Modifiers of the carriers in the Active Zone whatever its Visibility (ADR-0028's Known limit). The leak check compares Derived Reads with plain equality, so on a ruleset whose Active Zone is not public it would report the hidden carriers' contribution as a leak, which is the right verdict. No shipped ruleset has one (the Effects Ruleset's `field` is public; the Small Ruleset has no Active Zone), so this is **not exercised**, and no such ruleset is built. For the shipped ones the check does cover Derived Reads of visible cards under an active Field Modifier: the wiring test runs on a Game where one is active.

**Amended by ADR-0034:** the Relabelling also maps the cards of the `bound` names of a Paused Resolution and the card of a Card Answer, marked above.

**Observed Golden Games.** The Effects Leader-kill Golden Game as observed by each Player: `crates/tcg-loader/tests/golden/effects-leader-kill.observed-by-player-0.trace` and `-1.trace`, in ADR-0017's layout with Observed Events: a header, `== creation` and one `Debug` line per Observed Event of the creation trace, then per transition `== action N`, the `Debug` line of its Taken Action *(Amended by ADR-0033: written by `taken_line`, which renders a Taken Declared Action as the `Debug` line of the struct `TakenAction` was, so the snapshots are unchanged)* and one line per Observed Event, then `== outcome`. They are compared byte for byte, reporting the first differing line, and differ from each other; they are written only by the new ignored `regenerate_observed_golden_snapshots`. The existing snapshots and their regenerator are untouched. *(Amended by ADR-0035: the shipped Effects decks changed, so `effects-leader-kill.observed-by-player-1.trace` was regenerated, one line, the definition of a card seat 1 drew; the player-0 file is unchanged. A pausing pair, `effects-pausing.observed-by-player-0.trace` and `-1.trace`, joins them, written by the ignored `regenerate_pausing_golden_snapshots`.)*

**A handle that left sight.** `translate_action` of an Observed Action naming a handle the Viewer held for a card that has since left its sight is `UnknownObservedCard { card }`, pinned on a test-only Effects ruleset with `trash` hidden (a `pyre_imp` played, which its own trigger sends to the trash).

**Throughput, measured.** Release, `cargo run --release -p tcg-selfplay --example self_play`, 1,000 Games per cell, Games per second (Actions per second):

| Ruleset | Off | EngineOnly | On |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small | 195 (7,644) | 153 (6,024) | 19 (736) |
| Effects | 128 (5,886) | 107 (4,930) | 13 (603) |

Observing every Viewer at every step costs about 8 times the nine engine checks, the O(steps squared) Relabelled Game. It is a self-play and CI cost, never an engine cost; it is not optimised here (an incremental Relabelled Game is a later ticket if a measurement asks for it).

**The test profile.** With the observation checks on, the debug `cargo test --workspace` went from 2 min 37 s to about 9 min, nearly all of it the two 1,000-seed tests. `Cargo.toml` gains `[profile.test.package.tcg-engine]` and `[profile.test.package.tcg-selfplay]` with `opt-level = 2`: only the optimisation level of those two crates, and only under `cargo test`. `overflow-checks = true` stays on at the profile level and still applies to them, and there is still no float and no `unsafe`, so nothing about determinism changes. Measured: the debug `cargo test --workspace` takes 3 min 6 s with the override (compile included), against 2 min 37 s before the observation checks, and the `selfplay` test binary 94 s instead of 532 s. A probe under `cargo test` confirmed that an `u8` addition still panics with overflow in `tcg-selfplay`.

**Conflicts.** ADR-0020's "at most one violation per Invariant" becomes per (Invariant, Viewer), and its `Checks` gain `EngineOnly` (marked there). ADR-0026's "with checks on, self-play lists through it" also holds for `EngineOnly` (marked there). ADR-0028's Known limit is answered above (marked there). No other ADR changes.

**Status**: accepted
