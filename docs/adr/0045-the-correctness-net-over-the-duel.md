# The correctness net over the Duel

Self-play and a Golden Game now exercise the Duel Ruleset v3 on its shipped decks, under every Invariant of ADR-0020, ADR-0026 and ADR-0031 (ticket [43], #100, of the Phase G roadmap #94). It is ADR-0026's, ADR-0035's and ADR-0039's net applied to the Duel Ruleset, with ADR-0040's Seed Range mechanism. Test-only: nothing under `src/`, in the harness API, in the harness's `Checks` or `FailureKind`, or under `rulesets/duel-ruleset/*.yaml` changes, and no deck changes. The Uniform Random Policy, the Step Cap and `MAX_STEPS` are unchanged. Coverage is counted in the tests from the Game Record and the Event Traces, as `effect_use`, `choice_use` and `ResponseUse` are.

**Decks: kept.** Measured first, with the [42] decks as they are: every count below is at least 1 over the 1,000 pinned Seeds (the lowest, `deck_out_wins`, is 85 Games), so no card is swapped and `duel-ruleset.yaml`, the catalog and the decks do not change.

**No new Invariant, and the existing ones cover Player state.** Checked before writing a line, as ADR-0041 claimed it: no Invariant was extended.

**Coverage, counted over the 1,000 pinned Seeds in the same pass** as the Invariants (ADR-0040's mechanism: four Seed Ranges whose counts a joining test sums). `DuelUse` has ten counts: the ticket's eight and the two of ticket [41], which the superseded 100-Seed test asserted (`sets`, `position_changes`). Counts are of occurrences over all Games, except the two wins, which are of Games. Each is asserted at least 1 in a separate message naming it when it fails (`coverage count ties is 0 over Seeds 0..1000: {used:?}`), and then the whole struct is asserted equal to the values below by one `assert_eq!`, so a broken sum over the ranges fails and not only a count of 0. Never by `steps()`, which replays every Game. Measured:

|---|---|---|

**The Seed Ranges and `PARTS`.** The Duel's 1,000 Seeds are played in four contiguous ranges (`0..250` to `750..1000`), one test each, `duel_self_play_ends_every_game_over_seeds_<a>_to_<b>_with_every_invariant_holding`, plus `the_duel_seed_ranges_cover_seeds_0_to_1000_once` and the joining test `duel_self_play_covers_every_duel_use_and_matches_the_pin_over_1000_seeds`, which also asserts `longest * 2 < MAX_STEPS`. The mechanism is ADR-0040's, **duplicated** in `duel_games.rs` (`PARTS`, `seeds_of_range`, `SeedRangePlay`, `play_seed_range`, the `static SEED_RANGES: [OnceLock<..>; PARTS]`): two uses do not make a shared helper, and `response_games.rs` is untouched. Per Game each range asserts a Terminal Outcome, `TriggerCapRejections::Counted(0)`, no `FailureKind`, and `verify_record` on Seeds 0..100 (inside the range that holds them, `0..250`). The count was measured as ADR-0040 did, with the `selfplay` test binary on a machine that also runs other sessions, so the figures move by tens of seconds from run to run:

| Seed Ranges | `selfplay` binary (two runs) | Slowest Duel range, run alone |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | 89 s and 116 s | 38.4 s (`0..250`, which also holds the `verify_record` of Seeds 0..100; `750..1000` took 24.2 s) |
| 8 | 119 s and 117 s | 25.3 s (`0..125`) |

Eight was never faster than four, and the two are within noise, so four it is, as in ADR-0040 (the smallest count within noise of the best).

**The Duel Self-Play Pin.** `crates/tcg-selfplay/tests/selfplay/golden/duel-self-play-seeds-0-1000.digest`, the fourth pin, is in the format of ADR-0021's, ADR-0026's and ADR-0039's (`digest_line`), compared whole by the joining test over the digest lines of the ranges in Seed order, and rewritten only by the ignored `regenerate_duel_self_play_digests`, whose mismatch message names that command. The Small, Effects and Response pins, Golden Games and snapshots are byte-identical (their freeze digests still pass). **Retired:** `duel_self_play_ends_every_game_over_seeds_0_to_100_with_every_invariant_holding`: its Games are inside the 1,000, its `set_monster` and `change_position` assertions are the `sets` and `position_changes` counts, and its attack, tribute and spell assertions are covered by the eight counts. The determinism test, the replay tests, the deck-legality test and the observation test of `duel_games.rs` are unchanged.

**The Duel Golden Game.** `duel-direct-kill`, Seed 226, the shipped Duel decks, 22 Actions, in `crates/tcg-loader/tests/duel_golden_game.rs` (its cards named by constants). Seat 0 sets `quarry_bear` and later `pebble_golem`; seat 1's `reed_stalker` (ATK 900) attacks the set `quarry_bear` (DEF 1100), no card moves and seat 1 goes from 4,000 to 3,800 Life Points (damage to the attacker's owner from a higher DEF, Action 11); seat 0 summons `abyss_leviathan` (level 7, ATK 2600) by `tribute_summon_two` with `pebble_golem` and `quarry_bear` as tributes (Action 13) and attacks the `reed_stalker`, taking seat 1 to 2,100 (Action 15); seat 1 summons a `cinder_ogre` and seat 0 summons its own and attacks it, ATK 1700 against ATK 1700, a tie that destroys both (Action 21); seat 1 has no monster, so `abyss_leviathan` attacks directly for 2,600, seat 1 goes to -500 and the Game ends with one `life_out` Loss of seat 1 (Action 22). The Seed was found by a throwaway search (a goal-driven bot over Seeds 0..3000, in forty variants of its choices, each Game asking in turn for a two-tribute summon, a tie, damage from a higher DEF and a kill by direct attack, keeping the shortest Game that holds all four and ends by that kill; Seed 226 gave 22 Actions); the search is not committed, the literal script is the Game. **The script is kept exactly as the search found it**, plays the Game does not need included (seat 0's second set, seat 1's Battle phase without an attack): nobody should tidy it. Its snapshots are `crates/tcg-loader/tests/golden/duel-direct-kill.trace` and `duel-direct-kill.observed-by-player-0.trace` and `-1.trace`, in ADR-0017's and ADR-0031's layouts; the script holds only Declared Actions, so `golden.rs`'s renderer needed no change. They are compared byte for byte, reporting the first differing line; the two observed snapshots differ from each other. `the_duel_direct_kill_golden_game_holds_a_two_tribute_summon_a_tie_damage_from_a_higher_def_and_a_kill_by_direct_attack` asserts outside the snapshot the exact Events: the two tributes' `CardMoved` to the Graveyard before the summoned monster's move, the higher-DEF attack's whole trace (`attacked`, then Life Points 3,800, no `CardMoved`), the tie's whole trace (`attacked`, the target's move, the attacker's move, no `PlayerStateChanged`), the last `direct_attack`'s trace ending in `GameEnded` with one `life_out` Loss of seat 1 and a Life Points change to -500, and winners `[PlayerId(0)]`. One ignored regenerator, `regenerate_duel_golden_snapshots`, writes the three files and no other. `replaying_the_duel_direct_kill_golden_game_reproduces_it` builds the record from the script and replays it through the shared helper. The only change to the shared helpers: the `pass` helper (`pass_turn`) moved out of `golden.rs` into the two tests that use it, because the Duel has no `pass_turn` and an unused shared helper fails `-D warnings`; no other output changes.

**Step Cap.** The longest of the 1,000 Games is 225 Actions, so `longest * 2 < MAX_STEPS` (450 < 1,000) keeps its headroom and `MAX_STEPS` stays 1,000.

**Throughput, measured.** Release, `cargo run --release -p tcg-selfplay --example self_play`, 1,000 Games per cell, Games per second (Actions per second), ADR-0039's layout and a Duel row. All four rows were measured in this one run, so the first three differ from ADR-0039's by the machine:

| Ruleset | Off | EngineOnly | On |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small | 439 (17,261) | 252 (9,891) | 36 (1,397) |
| Effects | 188 (9,368) | 134 (6,681) | 20 (973) |
| Response | 182 (13,830) | 136 (10,306) | 13 (997) |
| Duel | 190 (22,369) | 130 (15,355) | 10 (1,215) |

Not optimised. The Duel Games are the longest of the four (117,819 Actions over 1,000 Games, against 75,929 for Response and 49,880 for Effects, longest 225), so Games per second with every check on are the lowest (10, 97 s per 1,000 Games) while Actions per second are the highest in every column (22,369 with checks off): the observation checks grow with the square of a Game's length (ADR-0031), and a Duel Game is long because a monster fight takes many Actions (summon, Battle, attack, End Turn).

**The test profile.** Measured as in ADR-0039, warm (everything compiled, then `cargo test --workspace` timed), on the same machine, which also runs other sessions: `main` 1 min 52 s, of which the `selfplay` test binary 96.8 s; this branch 2 min 49 s, of which `selfplay` 118.7 s (run alone, the binary took 89 s and 116 s with the 4-range layout). Before, the Duel ran 100 Seeds in one test; now it runs 1,000 Seeds in four parallel ranges, the slowest 38 s alone. The critical path of the binary is again `effects_self_play_ends_every_game_over_1000_seeds_with_every_invariant_holding` and the Small 1,000-Seed test beside the Response and Duel ranges, as ADR-0040 found.

**For [44]: the `selfplay` binary is about 2 minutes.** It takes 118.7 s here (89 to 119 s in the runs above), at or over the line of about 2 minutes that tickets [36] and [38] set. It is reported here and not optimised, as ticket [43] says. Its cost is the observation checks on every Seed of four rulesets, nearly all of it the Effects and Small 1,000-Seed tests, which are not split, and the Response and Duel ranges beside them. Options for [44], none taken here: splitting the Effects and Small tests into Seed Ranges with the same mechanism, an incremental Relabelled Game, or fewer Seeds under `Checks::On` with all 1,000 under `EngineOnly`.

**Conflicts, amended in place.** ADR-0017 (the Golden Games and their regenerators; the shared helpers), ADR-0020 and ADR-0026 (what self-play runs; the pins are four), ADR-0031 (observed Golden Games; the throughput table), ADR-0039 (the throughput table), and ADR-0041, ADR-0042, ADR-0043 and ADR-0044, whose out-of-scope sentences say the Duel pin, coverage counts, Golden Game and timings stay ticket [43]'s. Each carries an `Amended by ADR-0045:` line and the clause is marked where it stands. `CONTEXT.md` (*Golden Game*, new **Duel Golden Game**, *Seed Range*, new **Duel Coverage Counts**, *Self-Play Pin*) and `duel-ruleset.md` (the *Not yet* sentence, the glossary entries *Tie* and *Damage from a higher DEF*) are updated; no rule changes.

**Out of scope, by design.** New Invariants, other policies, optimisation (the cost of the `selfplay` binary included), new mechanics or cards, a deck change, any change to the Small, Effects or Response Rulesets, their pins, Golden Games or snapshots, a shared Seed Range helper, and a 1,000-Seed replay test.

**Status**: accepted
