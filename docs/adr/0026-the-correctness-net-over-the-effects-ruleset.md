Amended by ADR-0045: the Duel Ruleset has the same net over its Games: a fourth Self-Play Pin, coverage counts summed over four Seed Ranges by a joining test, and a Golden Game of its own. The Effects pin and Golden Games stay frozen.

Amended by ADR-0040: the Response Ruleset's 1,000 Seeds are played in four Seed Ranges, one test each, and its counts and pin are joined by a last test; the Effects test is unchanged. *(Superseded by ADR-0046: the Effects test is split the same way.)*

Amended by ADR-0046: the Effects Ruleset's 1,000 Seeds are played in Seed Ranges, one test each, and a last test sums the coverage counts, asserts them exactly and compares the Effects Self-Play Pin whole (marked in "The shipped Effects decks change" and "The Effects Self-Play Pin").

Amended by ADR-0037: the Effects Ruleset is frozen from Phase F's ticket [34], and asserted frozen by a digest test as the Small Ruleset is.

Amended by ADR-0036: `SelfPlayGame::trigger_cap_rejections` tells "none seen" (`Counted(0)`) from "not measured" (`NotMeasured`, with `Checks::Off`), and the state differences also compare the phase.

Amended by ADR-0035: the shipped Effects decks swap six vanilla creatures for `curious_owl`, `snare_vine` and `mind_leech`, so the Effects Golden Game, its observed snapshots and the Effects Self-Play Pin were regenerated, and the pin now holds Answers.

Amended by ADR-0033: a Game on can have no Legal Action again while a decision is pending, a trigger can ask for a decision so a record holds Answers, and the state differences also compare the Pending Decision.

Amended by ADR-0031: with checks on, self-play lists with rejections also under `Checks::EngineOnly`.

The harness of ADR-0020 now runs over the Effects Ruleset (ticket [20], #53), with two new Invariants, one listing capability in the engine, a counted outcome for the Trigger Cap, new sample decks and the freeze of the Small Ruleset asserted by a pinned digest. *(Amended by ADR-0037: the Effects Ruleset's freeze is asserted likewise, `the_effects_ruleset_is_frozen_since_phase_f`: ADR-0037)* The Uniform Random Policy, the Step Cap, the failure types (`FailureKind` is unchanged) and the use of the public API only are ADR-0020's, untouched.

**Two Invariants, after the seven.** `Invariant` gains `DerivedReadsDoNotLeak` and `RejectionLeavesStateIntact`, appended after the existing seven so their order and tests do not move. `check_opening` and `check_transition` keep their signatures and their seven; the new ones are checked by functions of their own, because they need what those do not have.

- `DerivedReadsDoNotLeak`, `check_recomputation(recomputed, next)`. Self-play and `verify_record` keep a **Recomputed GameState**: the Empty GameState (`GameCreation::empty`) with the opening trace applied, then each Action's trace, and they compare it, whole, with the engine's GameState at the Opening Position and after every transition. It is not `EventSourcedConsistency`: that check folds the trace from the engine's own prior GameState, so a value the engine carried forward outside an Event (a cached Field Modifier, which ADR-0024 and ADR-0025 forbid) would be in both sides and pass. Folding from the Empty GameState leaves nothing to carry. The violation's detail names the first part that differs (the Turn Number, the Active Player, a card instance and its state, a zone *(Amended by ADR-0033: and the Pending Decision)* *(Amended by ADR-0036: and the phase, after the Active Player; the detail names both phases)* *(Amended by ADR-0041: and a Player's Player state, naming the state and both values, before the zones)*). A fold that cannot be applied is itself this violation, not a panic. Over the Small Ruleset it is checked too and finds nothing.
- `RejectionLeavesStateIntact`, `check_rejection(ruleset, prior, action, rejection)`. A rejection carries no Event Trace and no GameState, so "emits nothing" holds by the type; the check runs the Action against `prior` again, requires the same Rejection and requires `prior` to equal a clone taken before the call (ADR-0002 makes this true by construction, so it has teeth only for a regression). Self-play runs it for each Trigger Cap Rejection it sees.

**`listing_with_rejections`.** To count what the Trigger Cap refused, self-play must see the candidates that `legal_actions` drops. `DeclarativeRuleset::listing_with_rejections(state)` returns every candidate in Listing Order with `Option<Rejection>`. This is the listing "that returns the transitions ... left for when a measurement asks for it" of ADR-0018. Both it and `legal_actions` call one private candidate walk, differing in whether a refused candidate is kept; `legal_actions` keeps none, so its common path allocates no rejected candidate (it builds one more `Vec` than before, of the accepted ones), runs the same `transition` per candidate and has the same order; the measured throughput shows no regression (ADR-0021 holds). The accepted subsequence of one is the other, which a test asserts, so the two cannot drift. With checks on, self-play lists through it and draws from the accepted subsequence *(Amended by ADR-0031: `Checks::EngineOnly` lists through it too, so that `On` and `EngineOnly` play the same Game and count the same Trigger Cap Rejections)*, so a Game is the same Game with checks on or off (a test asserts it); with checks off it uses `legal_actions`.

**The Trigger Cap is a counted outcome, not a failure.** ADR-0023 said that a Game with no Legal Action because of the cap would be `NoLegalAction`. That reading is unreachable: the Effects Ruleset always lists `pass_turn`, which resolves no trigger, so a Game on always has a Legal Action. *Amended by ADR-0033: a Game on can have no Legal Action again. While a decision is pending only its Answers are listed, and a pending decision whose every Answer is refused (each option wakes a trigger past the Trigger Cap) lists none: that is ADR-0020's `NoLegalAction`, a self-play failure like any other, with no special rule. The shipped catalog cannot reach it.* *(Amended by ADR-0037: with a Response Window open a Pass or a window action refused by the cap can likewise leave no Legal Action, and the Game reports `NoLegalAction` with no special rule; the shipped Response catalog cannot reach it.)* What self-play counts is the **Trigger Cap Rejections** seen while listing: candidates the cap refused, summed over every listing the policy drew from, in `SelfPlayGame::trigger_cap_rejections` (*Amended by ADR-0036: a `TriggerCapRejections`, `NotMeasured` with `Checks::Off`, which does not list with rejections, and `Counted(n)` otherwise, zero included; it was `0` with checks off*). A Game with such rejections is a good Game. `FailureKind` gets no new kind. With the shipped decks the count is `0` in every one of 1,000 Seeds (the decks hold no card that reaches the cap); over a deck that holds `restless_shade` it is not (a test pins 8 for one Seed and cross-checks it against a walk that does not use the listing).

**The shipped Effects decks change, and `pyre_imp` is added.** ADR-0023 said "the shipped decks are untouched, so the Small and Effects golden games stay equal". That is amended here: the point of this ticket is decks that play the effect cards. Deck A and deck B (`effects-ruleset.md`, Sample decks) are 50 creatures, at most 4 of each, and hold triggered, self-continuous and aura cards; every Card Instance Id after the first deck's start shifts, so the Effects Golden Game has its own script and snapshot, regenerated deliberately *(Amended by ADR-0035: the decks swap `iron_guard` 2, `wind_hawk` 2 and two of the four `ember_hound` for `snare_vine` 2, `mind_leech` 2 and `curious_owl` 2, in place in both decks, so deck sizes and every other Card Instance Id stay and the Leader-kill script is unchanged; the snapshot and the pin were regenerated)*. The equivalence ADR-0022 wanted to keep is kept on the Small Ruleset's cards: the Effects Ruleset plays the Small Ruleset's Leader-kill script with the Small sample decks into the same Event Traces (a test). No shipped card could produce a **cascade**, a trigger whose own Events wake another trigger, which the replay criterion names (only `restless_shade` moves a card in a `do`, and it is capped by design), so the catalog gains `pyre_imp`: `enter: field` moves it to the trash and `enter: trash` draws one card. The second effect draws and does not move the card back, so the chain has two steps. `restless_shade` stays out of the decks. Self-play turned up no dead or unreachable effect card: a one-off count over Seeds 0..1000 at this commit (not pinned per card) found each effect card played and its trigger acting (`grave_jackal` died 338 times), so no catalog card was changed beyond `pyre_imp`. What the tests do pin is per family: the pinned Effects Games are asserted to exercise the families, not only list them (at least 900 of 1,000 Games play a triggered card, 650 have an aura in the field and 700 have a self-continuous card in the field; measured 988, 759 and 795) *(Superseded by ADR-0046: the three minimums are gone. The three counts are asserted exactly over the 1,000 Seeds, 997, 770 and 819 with the decks of ADR-0035, by one `assert_eq!` of the summed counts)*.

**The Effects Self-Play Pin.** `crates/tcg-selfplay/tests/selfplay/golden/effects-self-play-seeds-0-1000.digest` is in the format of ADR-0021's pin, compared in the same pass as the invariants *(Amended by ADR-0046: compared whole by the test that joins the Seed Ranges, over the Games the Seed Range tests played)*, and rewritten only by the ignored `regenerate_effects_self_play_digests`. Replay of those records, cascades included, reproduces the Event Traces and the final GameStates exactly: a trigger asks for no decision, so a record is unchanged (ADR-0019, ADR-0023). *(Amended by ADR-0033: a trigger whose `do` holds a Choice asks for one, and its Answer is an Action of the record; the record keeps its shape and replays the Answers like any Action. No shipped deck holds such a card *(Superseded by ADR-0035: both shipped decks hold three, and the pin holds Answers of every kind)*.)*

*(Amended by ADR-0039: "The Effects Self-Play Pin" above is one of three *(Amended by ADR-0045: four; the Duel Ruleset's is `duel-self-play-seeds-0-1000.digest`, written only by the ignored `regenerate_duel_self_play_digests`)*. The Response Ruleset's is `response-self-play-seeds-0-1000.digest`, compared in the same pass as its Invariants and written only by the ignored `regenerate_response_self_play_digests`. *Amended by ADR-0040: the Response pin is compared whole by the test that joins the Seed Ranges, over the Games the Seed Range tests played, once.*)*

**The Small Ruleset freeze is asserted, not assumed.** `the_small_ruleset_is_frozen_since_phase_c` pins the FNV-1a digest of each of seven files: the Small ruleset, catalog and two decks, the two Small Golden Snapshots and the Small self-play pin, and names the file that changed. Pinning only the sources would let an accidental regeneration of a snapshot go unnoticed, because the regenerated file would match itself; the snapshots are pinned too. An edit to the frozen ruleset, or a regenerated Small snapshot, fails here. *(Amended by ADR-0037: the Effects Ruleset is frozen the same way from Phase F, by thirteen digests, and from then on the current phase's ruleset is the Response Ruleset)*

**What a failure's record reproduces.** `NoLegalAction` and the Step Cap verify as `Ok` and replay to the GameState the failure stopped in (ADR-0020), and the tests on the Effects Ruleset assert exactly that. `RejectionLeavesStateIntact` is found while listing, not by `verify_record`: its record replays to the GameState in which the Action was rejected. It cannot be produced through the public API.

**Throughput, measured.** `cargo run --release -p tcg-selfplay --example self_play`, 1,000 Games each (Seeds 0..1000): the Small Ruleset plays 301 Games/s and 11,811 Actions/s with checks off and 222 and 8,736 with checks on; the Effects Ruleset plays 177 Games/s and 8,140 Actions/s with checks off and 160 and 7,372 with checks on. Per Action, the Effects Ruleset costs about 1.45 times the Small Ruleset with checks off: the cost of the derived reads and the trigger scan, measured and not optimised (`modifier_sum`, `expression::read` and the listing are unchanged). The invariants, the Recomputed GameState included, add about 10 to 30 percent over the engine alone.

**Out of scope, by design.** Serialization and record files, policies other than the Uniform Random Policy, parallel Games, any optimisation, any change to the Small Ruleset, a new Card Effect grammar, matcher or primitive, and a change to the Trigger Cap's value or to `Rejection`'s shape. Conflict with an older ADR: ADR-0023's "the shipped decks are untouched", its five catalog creatures and its `NoLegalAction` reading of the cap are amended as above, and ADR-0022's "the golden games stay equal" is amended with them (each carries an "amended by" note); the rest of ADR-0018, ADR-0019, ADR-0020, ADR-0021, ADR-0022, ADR-0024 and ADR-0025 is unchanged.

**Status**: accepted
