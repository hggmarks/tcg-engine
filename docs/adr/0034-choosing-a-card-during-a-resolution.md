# Choosing a card during a Resolution

A Triggered Card Effect can ask a Player to pick one card from a set, and the rest of its `do` reads the card by name (ticket [30], #76; checkpoint #67 decisions). It is the second kind of Choice, on the pause, the Answer and the Deciding Player of ADR-0033, and on the Param machinery of ADR-0015, ADR-0016 and ADR-0032, which it reuses and does not reimplement.

**The Card Choice.** The Action Effect `choose_card: { name, decider, zone, card_type, owner?, where? }` or `{ name, decider, one_of: [...] }` (`Effect::ChooseCard { decider, param }`, `EffectDraft::ChooseCard { decider, param: ParamDraft }`). The spec **is** a `Param`: it is read as a `ParamDraft`, checked by `validate_param` and held as a `Param`, whose name is the bound name. `decider` is `controller` or `opponent`, as for the Option Choice (`opponent` needs exactly 2 Players: `OpponentDeciderNeedsTwoPlayers` at `choose_card.decider`, found in the same walk as `choose_one`'s). The loader reads the keys of a param plus `decider` (the param grammar's parsing is one helper for both) and refuses unknown keys. Placement is that of the Option Choice: only a Triggered Card Effect's `do`, also nested in an `if` or an option; elsewhere `ChoiceOutsideTriggeredEffect` at `choose_card`, found by the absence of `self`.

**`owner` is relative to the controller.** The candidates are those of an action param for an acting Player, and the acting Player is the **controller** (the owner of `self`), never the decider: absent `owner` means the controller's zone, `owner: opponent` the controller's opponent's, whoever decides. `mind_leech` (decider `opponent`, `owner: opponent`) therefore picks from its own hand.

**Lexical scope and Bound Names.** `name` is a Card Ref, and its fields and instance states Value Refs, for the effects after the Choice in the same list and in everything nested in them (an `if`, an option); it is not in scope once the enclosing `if` or option ends. A name is unique in scope: one already in scope, `self` included, is `DuplicateChoiceName { name }` at `choose_card.name`; siblings (`then` and `else`, two options) and a later list that the first one no longer reaches may reuse a name. `validate_effects` threads the scope: after a Choice at index `i`, the effects `i+1..` are checked with the name added as a `ScopedParam` of the alternatives' card types, so `if is_card_type` narrows it (ADR-0032). The checks of a Choice run in this order: outside a Card Effect, the name (`InvalidId`, then `DuplicateChoiceName`), the decider (`UnknownDecider`), the param as for an action (its `where` reads `self`, the earlier names and its own candidate), then the visibility below.

**The Decider sees every candidate, checked at load.** For each alternative in order, its zone is `public`, or `owner` with that zone's owner the decider: `owner` absent with `decider: controller`, or `owner: opponent` with `decider: opponent`. Otherwise `DeciderCannotSeeZone { zone }` at `choose_card.zone` or `choose_card.one_of[i].zone`. A `hidden` zone is never seen. Choosing from an unseen zone is a peek (look, reveal), which the Card Choice does not do and a later ticket would decide.

**Candidates.** `arguments::candidates(ruleset, state, controller, scope, param)` composes the existing pieces, as `fillings` does: the cards of `candidate_cards` that `bind_param` accepts with the controller as `actor` and `scope` (`self` and the earlier names) readable by the `where`, in ascending Card Instance Id (ADR-0018's order). They are evaluated when the Choice is reached, on the GameState of that moment, and **recomputed** when the Answer arrives, from `self` and the bound names the Paused Resolution keeps; `DecisionKind::Card` stores nothing.

**No candidate, one candidate.** With none, nobody is asked, no Event is emitted, and the remaining effects of **that `do`**, the Triggered Card Effect's key, are skipped, the enclosing `if`s and options included: earlier effects stand. The Resolution goes on (the Events emitted so far are scanned, the queue continues): `Flow::Skipped` beside `Done` and `Paused` is propagated up without running anything else, and is a completed `do` at the trigger level, in `fire` and in `resume` alike. With exactly one candidate the decision is still asked. *Amends ADR-0023:* a `do` can end early.

**Binding happens on resume.** A straight run never binds a name, since a Card Choice always pauses or skips. `PausedResolution` gains `bound: Vec<BoundName>`, `BoundName { name: ParamName, card }`: the names in scope at the Choice in binding order, `self` left out (`self` is `trigger.card`). A pause inside a resumed `do` keeps the names of the levels it entered (`entered` keeps them). A resume **rebuilds the scope per level**, exactly: at each list, the outer scope plus, for each Card Choice in `list[..e]` (`e` the branch effect, or the Choice at the innermost level), its name with the card found in `paused.bound` (a missing name is `CardDataMismatch`); the Answer's card is bound at the innermost level, and the outer list goes on with the outer level's scope, so a name bound inside a branch never reaches the list that follows it and a later Choice that reuses it reads its own card.

**The Answer.** `Answer::Card(CardInstanceId)`, `DecisionKind::Card`. The new Rejections are `WrongAnswerKind { kind }` (the pending kind) and `CardNotCandidate { card }`. The checks of an Answer run in this order: `NoDecisionPending`, the kind (`WrongAnswerKind`), then `OptionOutOfRange` or `CardNotCandidate` (not in the recomputed list, an id no card has included), then the rejections of running it; an inconsistency is `CardDataMismatch`. A Rejection checks before `DecisionAnswered` is applied, so it leaves the GameState untouched and emits no Event.

**Listing.** A pending Card Choice lists `Answer::Card(c)` for each candidate of the Deciding Player in order, each through `transition`; `listing_with_rejections` alike.

**Observing.** `ObservedAnswer::Card(TakenArgument)` names the card by what the Viewer sees, and the two places that carry an Answer read it by their own rule. The **Observed `DecisionAnswered`** is read from the Viewer's sight at that Event, as every Observed Event is: the handle the Observer holds when the Event is folded (`DecisionAnswered` changes no sight), else `Card(HiddenCard)`. So `follow_events` gives the same Observed Events however a trace is sliced: a slice of several transitions never names a card by a handle the Viewer gets only later. The **Taken Action** (`follow_transition` only) uses the Taken Action rule, exactly as for the card arguments of a Declared Action, which no Observed Event carries either: the Observed Card the Viewer holds after the transition, else the one it held before, else `HiddenCard`. The two values coincide whenever the Viewer sees the card before the Answer, the decider always does (the load check above), and differ for a Viewer that first sees the card in the same transition: a card that moves from a hand it cannot see to a public trash is `HiddenCard` in the Observed Event and `Card(h)` in the Taken Action, and one that stays in that hand is `HiddenCard` in both. `observed_legal_actions` lists `Answer::Card(id)` as `Card(TakenArgument::Card(handle))`, a missing handle being `ObserverOutOfStep` (the decider sees every candidate, so it holds one); `translate_action` maps a handle back or gives `UnknownObservedCard`, and `Card(HiddenCard)`, which names nothing, gives `AnswerNamesHiddenCard`. The Observed Pending Decision shows the kind `Card` and never the Paused Resolution or its bound names.

**Relabelling.** The self-play Relabelling of ADR-0031 maps the cards of `paused.bound` and the card of a `DecisionAnswered { answer: Answer::Card(c) }` through the same permutation, and every place Actions are relabelled maps `Answer::Card`.

**Catalog.** `snare_vine` (creature, cost 2, power 1): on entering the field the controller picks a creature in an opponent's field and taps it. `mind_leech` (creature, cost 2, power 2): on entering the field the opponent picks a creature from their own hand and it goes to their trash. In the catalog only; the shipped decks change in [31]. The 3-Player ruleset of one aura test no longer loads the full real catalog (`mind_leech` needs 2 Players), and the test removes that card from its catalog text.

**No golden file changes.** `PausedResolution` gains a field, which changes the `Debug` of `DecisionPending`; no shipped Golden Game or pin holds a pause, so no `.digest` or `tests/golden/` file changes.

**Conflicts, amended in place.** ADR-0033 (the Answer, `DecisionKind`, the Paused Resolution, the rejections and their order, the listing, the resume, the Observed Answer and Taken Action, the Relabelling and "the Card Choice is a later ticket's"), ADR-0015 and ADR-0016 (a param is also the spec of a Card Choice, with the controller acting), ADR-0022 (`self` is no longer the only name in scope of a Card Effect), ADR-0018 (the listing while pending), ADR-0023 (a `do` can end early), ADR-0029 (the Taken Action's Answer and the row of `DecisionAnswered`), ADR-0030 (the Observed Answer, the listing, §4's handle-only listing and the translation) and ADR-0031 (the Relabelling) each carry an `Amended by ADR-0034` line and mark the affected clause where it stands. ADR-0032's overflow rule applies to a Card Choice unchanged: an overflowing `where` of a `one_of` alternative means it does not fit.

**Status**: accepted
