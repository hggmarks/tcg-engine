# Continuous Card Effects over matched sets

**The decision.** A Continuous Card Effect may carry a **Modifier Scope**, `scope: { zone, card_type, owner?, include_self? }`, beside its `while` and `modify`. Its Field Modifier then adds `amount` to the derived read of `modify.field` of every card the scope matches, instead of (or besides) the carrying card alone. Like the modifier of [18] it is a derived read (ADR-0024): nothing is stored, no Event is emitted, and `expression::read` is the one read path. Without a `scope` the behavior of ADR-0024 is unchanged.

**The grammar is a filter, not an expression.** `zone` (a declared zone) and `card_type` (a declared card type) are required: an absent `card_type` would leave "matches every type" undecided, and the criterion cards need both. `owner` is absent, meaning the carrying card's owner (`ScopeOwner::Controller`), or `opponent`, meaning *any* Player other than the carrying card's owner (`ScopeOwner::Opponent`, the relation `owner: opponent` already has in ADR-0016; not "the other player", so it holds beyond two Players). `include_self` is absent or false, or true; it matters only when `owner` is absent, because the carrier is never in the opponent relation to itself, so `owner: opponent` with `include_self: true` is accepted and changes nothing (inert, not invalid, so no rejection: every accepted form still executes). The `owner` key has two bases: a param's is relative to the acting Player (ADR-0016), a scope's to the carrier's owner. A card is reached when it is in `zone` of its owner, its Card Definition's type is `card_type`, its owner stands in the `owner` relation to the carrier's owner, and it is not the carrier unless `include_self`. There is no per-target `where` Expression, because nothing in the criteria needs one and keeping Expressions out of the match is what keeps derived reads non-recursive: the match reads no field of any card, only zone, type and owner, so a derived read never needs another derived read. `while` stays required (`while: true` for an always-on aura), is evaluated with base values only on the carrier as `self` (ADR-0024), and `scope` alone with `modify` and no `while` is `MissingCardEffectShape`; a `scope` beside `on`, `if` or `do` is `MixedCardEffectShapes`.

**The carrier is in the Active Zone; the targets are where the scope says.** A modifier is active while its carrying card is in the ruleset's Active Zone (ADR-0024, unchanged) and its `while` holds. The scope's `zone` names where its targets are and may be any declared zone, so "modifiers from zones other than the Active Zone" stays out of scope in the sense of the carrier only. With no Active Zone declared no set modifier is active either.

**Where it lives.** `declarative::modifiers::modifier_sum(ruleset, state, card, field)` is the seam ADR-0024 named, and the only code that changes in the engine's read path: its signature is unchanged, and so are `expression::read` and every caller. It adds the own modifiers of `card` (no scope, `card` in the Active Zone), then scans the carriers in the Active Zone in Player Order and, within a Player, in stored zone order, for the set-scoped modifiers that reach `card` for `field`, cheap filters (field, zone, type, owner, self) before the `while`. Modifiers are additive, so no ordering rule among them is needed; the iteration order is still fixed (ADR-0004) so errors are deterministic. Nothing clamps a modified int: a derived power of 0 or below is reachable and a combat stays decided by `gte`. Overflow in the sum or in the final addition is `DerivedValueOverflow { card, field }` where `card` is the card being read, not a carrier; a card whose definition is missing is `CardDataMismatch`.

**Validation.** `scope` is checked after `while` and before `modify`: zone (`DanglingReference` at `scope.zone`), card type (`DanglingReference` at `scope.card_type`), owner (`UnknownOwner` at `scope.owner`, reusing the `DefinitionErrorKind` of ADR-0016 through `InvalidCardEffect`). The scope's card type needs no `effects: true`: its targets carry nothing. `modify.field` is checked against the scope's card type, not the carrier's, when there is a scope (an aura on a type with no `power` can still grant `power`; `UnknownModifyField` and `ModifyFieldNotInt` at `modify.field`, as before), and against the carrier's type otherwise.

**Nothing well-formed is rejected as unsupported.** This lifts the last surface of ADR-0022's "unsupported until implemented": every form the loader accepts executes, and the scope's only rejections are ordinary validation (dangling zone, dangling card type, unknown owner, a bad `modify.field`) and syntax (an unknown key inside `scope`, a syntax error like `modify`'s).

**Out of scope, by design.** Non-additive modifiers and layering, modifiers carried from outside the Active Zone, modifying instance states, a per-target `where`, scopes over players or counts, and new primitives, actions or trigger matchers. Conflict with an older ADR: none beyond the widening ADR-0024 predicted for `modifier_sum`; ADR-0002, ADR-0010 and ADR-0016 are untouched (no Event, no new state, the same refs).

**Status**: accepted
