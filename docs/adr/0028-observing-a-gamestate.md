# Observing a GameState

Amended by ADR-0037: the Stack, the Player with Priority and the Deciding Player's new rule are always visible.

Amended by ADR-0033: the Observed GameState also holds the Observed Pending Decision.

Amended by ADR-0031: the Known limit paragraph is answered (a non-public Active Zone is not exercised).

Amended by ADR-0029: `ObserverOutOfStep` also covers an Event of a trace that does not apply to the GameState it is followed from, or a transition whose GameState is not the one its trace leads to.

Amended by ADR-0030: two `ObservationError` variants are appended (`ActionOfAnotherPlayer`, `LegalActionNamesUnseenCard`), and `observed_legal_actions` also raises `ObserverOutOfStep`.

The first half of hidden information (ticket [23], #63, of the Observations roadmap #61): for one Viewer, an Observed GameState of a declarative Game, built by an Observer that folds the Events, with Observed Cards in place of Card Instance Ids and Derived Reads of the cards the Viewer sees. Observed Events and the incremental form arrive in [24], Observed Legal Actions in [25].

**The capability.** `Observations: Ruleset` is an optional trait beside `LegalActions` (ADR-0018), with associated types `Viewer`, `Observer`, `ObservedState` and `ObservationError` and two methods: `observer(viewer, empty, events)`, the Observer of the Viewer once `events` were applied, in order, to the Game's Empty GameState; and `observe(observer, state)`, the Observed GameState of the GameState the Observer has followed. The Declarative Ruleset implements it; the Toy Ruleset does not (a `compile_fail` doctest pins it, next to a passing one for the Declarative Ruleset, as ADR-0018 did). `Ruleset` and the event model are unchanged, there is no serde and no I/O, and the Observation never changes the GameState, the Events, the Game Record or a golden snapshot.

**Viewer.** `Viewer::Player(PlayerId)` is the only kind, in a non-exhaustive enum so that a Spectator, a later kind, is additive. A Viewer who is not a Player of the Game is `UnknownViewer`.

**Visibility.** The exact meaning of ADR-0004's three values: `public` is seen by every Viewer, `owner` by the owner of the zone only, `hidden` by nobody, its owner included. A Viewer sees the cards of a zone it can see, each with its Observed Card, Card Definition and instance state as stored; of a zone it cannot see it sees only the count and never the order. Always visible: every zone's count, the Turn Number, the Active Player, the phase and the Terminal Outcome *(Amended by ADR-0037: and the Stack: each Stack Item's controller, source and bound cards as Observed Cards, and the Player with Priority)* *(Amended by ADR-0033: and whether a decision is pending, its Deciding Player and its kind, never the Paused Resolution)*. Never: the Random State, the order of an unseen zone, a Card Instance Id. This meets the bar ADR-0010 set (no `random_state`, no order of a hidden zone, no way to link the id of a hidden card to its definition): an observed type holds none of them, and a card that comes into sight from an unseen zone gets a fresh handle, so a handle says nothing about where the card was.

**Observed Card.** A per-Viewer handle `ObservedCard(u32)`, dense from 0 in the order cards become visible to that Viewer, in Event order (not id order or zone order: for seat 0 of the Effects Ruleset, the two Leaders, then the five drawn cards in draw order, then the first two mana). A handle is retired, never reused, when its card leaves the Viewer's sight; a card that comes back is a new handle (pinned by tests in [24]).

**Observer.** A per-Viewer pure fold: it keeps the maps card to handle and handle to card and the next handle, is never shown to the Viewer, and exposes `viewer`, `card_instance(handle)` and `observed_card(card)`, the mapping [25] reads. It folds every Event applied to the Empty GameState so far, in order: the creation trace first and then, when a caller has them, each accepted transition's trace. One total rule per Event: a card **appears** (gets the next handle) when a `CardCreated` puts it, or a `CardMoved` brings it from an unseen zone, into a zone the Viewer sees; it **disappears** when a `CardMoved` takes it from a seen zone to an unseen one; otherwise it keeps its handle. Every other Event leaves the mapping alone. The fold is `Observer::follow(ruleset, event)`, crate-private, which returns what the Event did to the card's sight (`Sight`), for [24] to project from; [23] builds the Observer with it and tests only its own criteria.

**The Observed GameState.** `ObservedGameState` has the Viewer, the Players in seat order, the Active Player, the Turn Number, the phase, the Terminal Outcome, *(Amended by ADR-0033: and the Observed Pending Decision, `pending_decision()`, with the decider and the kind and never the Paused Resolution, and `deciding_player()`)* for each Player its `ObservedZone`s in declared order (a count, and `cards()` only for a seen zone, bottom first), and the `VisibleCard`s by ascending handle (Observed Card, owner, Card Definition, stored instance state). *(Amended by ADR-0037: plus `stack()` and `priority()`, and `deciding_player()` is the Decider, else the Player with Priority, else the Active Player)* It holds base facts only: no derived value. `observe` fails with `ObserverOutOfStep` if a seen card has no handle or the Observer holds a handle for a card that is not in a seen zone.

**Derived Read.** It is an inherent method of the Declarative Ruleset, `derived_read(observer, state, card, field)`, like `listing_with_rejections`, not a method of the trait: the trait carries only what every observing Ruleset has. It reads an int field of a card the Viewer sees through the engine's derived read (ADR-0024, ADR-0025), so the active Field Modifiers, self and aura, are included. Its checks, in order: `UnknownViewer`, `UnknownObservedCard` (the handle is not held), the card is absent from the GameState or in an unseen zone (`ObserverOutOfStep`), `NotAnIntField` (no int field of that name on the card's definition), then the read; an overflow is `DerivedValueOverflow` naming the Observed Card.

**Errors.** `ObservationError` is `UnknownViewer`, `UnknownObservedCard`, `NotAnIntField`, `DerivedValueOverflow`, `ObserverOutOfStep` and `ObservedCardOverflow` (the handle counter is checked, never wraps). *Amended by ADR-0030: `ActionOfAnotherPlayer` and `LegalActionNamesUnseenCard` are appended to the variants.* No variant carries a Card Instance Id or a Random State, and nothing panics on any input. `ObserverOutOfStep` is the one error for Events and a GameState that do not fit the Observer or the Ruleset: a `CardMoved` out of a zone the Viewer sees of a card the Observer has no handle for (or out of an unseen zone of a card it holds one for); a `CardCreated` of a card that already has a handle; a zone the Ruleset does not declare; and, in `observe` and `derived_read`, a seen card without a handle, a handle without a seen card, a card missing from the GameState or its catalog. *Amended by ADR-0030: `observed_legal_actions` also gives `ObserverOutOfStep` for a seen card without a handle or a card missing from the GameState; a card in a zone the Viewer cannot see is `LegalActionNamesUnseenCard` there, whether or not a handle is held.* *Amended by ADR-0029: it is also the error for an Event of a trace that does not apply to the GameState the Observer follows it from, or a transition whose GameState is not the one its trace leads to.*

**Honest limits.** The Observer trusts the Events: it does not check them against the Empty GameState (that is `apply`'s job), only against its own mapping and the declared zones. *Amended by ADR-0029: `follow_events` and `follow_transition` apply each Event to the previous GameState, so an Event that does not apply is `ObserverOutOfStep`.* A count is always visible, so a Viewer sees that a hidden zone changed size; and a seen zone keeps its stored order, which for an `owner` zone (the hand) is shown to its owner. Both are the rules, not leaks.

**Known limit for [26]'s leak check.** *(Answered by ADR-0031: the leak check compares Derived Reads with plain equality, so on a ruleset with a non-public Active Zone it would report the hidden carriers' contribution as a leak, which is the right verdict; no shipped ruleset has one, so it is not exercised.)* A Derived Read adds the Field Modifiers of every carrier in the Active Zone, whatever that zone's Visibility. The Effects Ruleset's Active Zone is the public `field`, and the Small Ruleset has none, so nothing hidden contributes. A ruleset with a non-public Active Zone would let a visible card's Derived Read reveal that hidden carriers exist. This ticket does not change it; the self-play leak check of [26] must account for it.

**Conflicts.** ADR-0004's recorded open item "hidden information is not yet decided" is decided here and marked as amended there. ADR-0010's rule for future observations is met, not changed. ADR-0018's capability pattern is reused unchanged.

**Status**: accepted
